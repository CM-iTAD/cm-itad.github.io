Computational Methods and Intelligent Technology in Architecture and Design

Position

CM-iTAD is Alfaisal University's applied research hub at the intersection of architecture, robotics, and artificial intelligence. We translate emerging computational methods into practical tools that make buildings safer, faster to construct, more sustainable, and more responsive to how people actually live.

The lab works at three linked scales. At material and machine scale, robotic fabrication and additive manufacturing ask what can actually be built, and how efficiently. At building scale, graph machine learning and simulation ask how a design will perform before it exists. At urban scale, thermal and density studies ask what those buildings do to the city around them.

Research runs alongside teaching. Students work on the same arms, printers and pipelines as the lab's funded projects, which keeps the research grounded and gives graduates experience that transfers directly into construction and design technology.
